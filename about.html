About us

A car wash that comes to you — done right.

AquaShine started with a simple frustration: great car care shouldn't require giving up your Saturday morning. So we built the wash around your schedule, not the other way around.

Our story

Detailing-grade care, without the detailing-shop hassle.

Every AquaShine van is a rolling studio: spot-free water, professional machines, and a full range of pH-neutral, biodegradable products. Our specialists are trained in paint-safe techniques — the two-bucket method, foam pre-soak, and machine polishing — so your car is never at the mercy of a spinning brush.

We serve busy professionals, parents, and car lovers who want their vehicle cared for while they work, rest, or spend time with family.

What we stand for

Three promises, on every job.

Quality first

Paint-safe methods, professional products, and a final walk-around with you. If something isn't right, we fix it on the spot — free.

Gentle on the planet

Biodegradable soaps, water-reclamation mats, and up to 80% less water than a driveway wash. Great shine shouldn't cost the earth.

Respect for your time

On-time arrival in a 30-minute window, or 20% off your wash. Book in a minute, pay after you're happy.

Ready when you are.

Join thousands of drivers who never visit a car wash anymore.
